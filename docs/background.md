## One-liner
Building a sound pulse which works like a QR code: sending users create a pulse and associated website and receiving users are prompted to go to a specified website upon receiving the pulse.

## Problem
For large events, QR codes can be inconvenient, becauses users need to repeatedly pull out their camera (which might be broken) and scan the websites. Furthermore, laptops do not have cameras and therefore URLs must be typed individually.
